    Lifecycle chapter 7

    Execution and Team Leadership

    Execution is concurrent coordination: the team creates results while learning, communicating, engaging, resolving conflict, implementing responses, and producing performance evidence.

    Lifecycle orientation

    Process Phases

    These process groups overlap and repeat; the emphasis shows where this chapter most often concentrates.

    1. InitiatingProcess group
    2. PlanningProcess group
    3. ExecutingProcess group · emphasized
    4. Monitoring and ControllingProcess group · emphasized
    5. ClosingProcess group

    Local sequence

    1. Confirm authorized work, near-term priorities, resources, decision rights, and working agreements.
    2. Direct and manage project work to create deliverables and work performance data.
    3. Acquire, develop, and support the team; address conflict at the appropriate level.
    4. Manage knowledge so experience can improve present work and future decisions.
    5. Perform quality, communications, risk-response, procurement, and stakeholder-engagement work concurrently.
    6. Route issues, impediments, defects, new risks, and proposed changes into their controlled decision paths.

    Handoffs

    What enters

    Authorized plans and changes, resources, stakeholder expectations, agreements, risk responses, and quality criteria.

    What this work accomplishes

    Creates deliverables, team capability, communications, knowledge, implemented responses, and observed performance.

    What moves next

    Completed work enters verification; data enters domain controls; issues and changes enter decisions; learning returns to current plans.

    Leadership, conflict, and feedback

    The project manager integrates work and removes organizational friction within delegated authority. Team members own assigned work and contribute expertise. Functional managers or resource owners retain relevant people authority. Sponsors and governance bodies resolve escalated direction or funding decisions. Stakeholders participate through the planned engagement routes.

    Conflict is information about competing needs, constraints, or behavior. Resolve it at the lowest responsible level that has authority and context; escalate when safety, ethics, compliance, contractual limits, or delegated authority require it. An impediment should have an owner and path to removal even though the repository does not yet have a source-verified canonical impediment artifact page.

    Approach treatment and artifact states

    Predictive execution coordinates against approved work packages and plans. Adaptive execution selects near-term items, collaborates through short cycles, demonstrates increments, and learns from feedback. Hybrid execution must keep cross-cadence dependencies and decision rights visible.

    Work performance data are observations from execution. They become Work Performance Information only after analysis. Completed deliverables are not verified or accepted merely because the team finished production.

    Exam distinctions

    Approved changes are implemented; proposed changes are not. Manage Project Knowledge is continuous rather than a final archive task. Team development and team management are related but distinct. Servant leadership does not remove accountability, governance, or escalation.
