    Tools and Techniques

    This grouped reference explains the canonical techniques connected to the Atlas’s 49-process predictive scaffold. The scaffold is supplemental: it is not the July 2026 exam hierarchy, a lifecycle made of five consecutive phases, or a mandatory method. Select and tailor techniques to the delivery context, evidence need, and delegated authority.

    Coverage: 138 canonical technique records across 19 families. Every source-listed item from the registered supplemental guide has a recorded disposition; family headings are consolidated rather than duplicated as techniques.

    Current-course support is stated only where a verified lesson locator exists. “Supplemental predictive evidence” describes the source of the locator—not a claim that the technique is incompatible with adaptive or hybrid delivery.
