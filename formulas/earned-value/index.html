    Earned Value Management

    Compare authorized planned value, budgeted value earned, and actual cost at one status date, then forecast using an explicit assumption.

    PredictiveAdaptiveHybrid

    The three measures

    PV — Planned Value: authorized budget assigned to work scheduled for completion by the status date.

    EV — Earned Value: authorized budgeted value of work actually completed by the status date. A simple example may calculate EV as percent complete × budget for the measured work, but the measured budget may be a project, control account, work package, or other defined performance scope—EV is not merely “a percentage of BAC.”

    AC — Actual Cost: actual cost incurred for the completed work by the status date.

    BAC — Budget at Completion: approved cost baseline at completion for the measured scope.

    !!! note "Schedule variance uses cost units" SV and CV use cost units because PV, EV, and AC are monetary budget values. SV is not directly a number of calendar days ahead or behind schedule.

    Formula reference

    Verified · current 2026 courseFRM-EVM-001

    Schedule variance

    SV = EV - PV

    PredictiveHybrid

    Interpretation: Positive is ahead of planned value; zero is on plan; negative is behind planned value.

    Assumptions: EV and PV cover the same measured scope and status date.

    Units: Currency or other budget units.

    schedule variance in cost units
    EV
    earned value

    Exam note: SV is expressed in cost units, not calendar days.

    Verified · current 2026 courseFRM-EVM-002

    Schedule performance index

    SPI = EV / PV

    PredictiveHybrid

    Interpretation: Above 1 is ahead of planned value; 1 is on plan; below 1 is behind planned value.

    Assumptions: PV is nonzero and scope/status date are aligned.

    Units: Dimensionless ratio.

    Exam note: SPI does not prove a specific number of days ahead or behind.

    Cost variance

    CV = EV - AC

    PredictiveHybrid

    Interpretation: Positive is under budget; zero is on budget; negative is over budget.

    Assumptions: EV and AC cover the same measured scope and status date.

    Units: Currency or other budget units.

    Verified · current 2026 courseFRM-EVM-004

    Cost performance index

    CPI = EV / AC

    PredictiveHybrid

    Interpretation: Above 1 is cost efficient; 1 is on budget; below 1 is cost inefficient.

    Assumptions: AC is nonzero and scope/status date are aligned.

    Units: Dimensionless ratio.

    CPI
    cost performance index
    EV

    AC

    Exam note: CPI below 1 means the project earns less budgeted value per unit of actual cost.

    Verified · current 2026 courseFRM-EVM-005

    Estimate at completion — current CPI continues

    EAC = BAC / CPI

    PredictiveHybrid

    Interpretation: Forecasts total cost if current cost efficiency continues.

    Assumptions: Current cumulative cost efficiency is expected to continue for the remaining work; CPI is nonzero.

    Verified · current 2026 courseFRM-EVM-006

    Estimate to complete

    ETC = EAC - AC

    PredictiveHybrid

    Interpretation: Forecasts cost remaining from the status date.

    Assumptions: EAC and AC use the same measured scope and status date.

    Units: Currency.

    ETC
    forecast remaining cost
    EAC
    forecast total cost

    actual cost to date

    Exam note: EAC is total forecast; ETC is remaining forecast.

    EAC — remaining work at original rate

    EAC = AC + (BAC - EV)

    PredictiveHybrid

    Interpretation: Adds actual cost to the original budget for uncompleted work.

    Assumptions: Past variance is atypical and remaining work will perform at the original budgeted rate.

    EAC — cost and schedule influence remaining work

    EAC = AC + ((BAC - EV) / (CPI × SPI))

    Interpretation: Adjusts remaining work for both cost and schedule performance.

    Assumptions: Both current cost and schedule performance are expected to influence remaining cost; CPI and SPI are nonzero.

    Units: Currency.

    Verified · current 2026 courseFRM-EVM-009

    EAC — bottom-up remaining estimate

    EAC = AC + bottom-up ETC

    PredictiveHybrid

    Interpretation: Replaces statistical extrapolation with a new detailed remaining-work estimate.

    Assumptions: The original estimate is no longer reliable and the remaining work has been re-estimated.

    Units: Currency.

    EAC
    forecast total cost
    AC
    actual cost
    bottom-up ETC
    new detailed estimate of remaining work

    Exam note: Bottom-up ETC is an input from re-estimating, not a value derived from CPI.

    Supplemental · official PMIFRM-EVM-010

    Variance at completion

    VAC = BAC - EAC

    PredictiveHybrid

    Interpretation: Positive forecasts under BAC; zero forecasts at BAC; negative forecasts over BAC.

    Assumptions: BAC and EAC cover the same measured scope.

    Units: Currency.

    VAC
    variance at completion
    BAC
    Budget at Completion
    EAC
    forecast total cost

    Exam note: Supplemental coverage; do not assume equal course emphasis.

    Supplemental · official PMIFRM-EVM-011

    To-complete performance index against BAC

    TCPI(BAC) = (BAC - EV) / (BAC - AC)

    PredictiveHybrid

    Interpretation: Efficiency the remaining work must achieve to meet BAC.

    Assumptions: BAC minus AC is positive and BAC remains the selected target.

    Units: Dimensionless ratio.

    TCPI
    required future cost efficiency
    BAC
    Budget at Completion
    EV
    earned value
    AC
    actual cost

    Exam note: Compare the required efficiency with demonstrated performance for realism.

    Supplemental · official PMIFRM-EVM-012

    To-complete performance index against approved EAC

    TCPI(EAC) = (BAC - EV) / (EAC - AC)

    PredictiveHybrid

    Interpretation: Efficiency the remaining work must achieve to meet the approved EAC target.

    Assumptions: EAC minus AC is positive and EAC is the selected approved target.

    Units: Dimensionless ratio.

    TCPI
    required future cost efficiency
    BAC
    Budget at Completion
    EV
    earned value
    EAC
    approved forecast target
    AC
    actual cost

    Exam note: The numerator remains budgeted work remaining; the denominator changes to funds remaining against EAC.

    EVM status-date chart

    ServiceBridge worked example

    Inputs: BAC $2,400,000; PV $1,200,000; EV $1,000,000; AC $1,250,000.

    Measure Result Meaning
    SV $-200,000 Less budgeted work completed than planned
    SPI 0.8333 Behind planned-value progress
    CV $-250,000 Actual cost exceeds earned value
    CPI 0.8000 $0.80 of budgeted value earned per $1.00 spent
    EAC = BAC / CPI $3,000,000 Forecast if current cost efficiency continues
    ETC $1,750,000 Forecast cost remaining
    VAC $-600,000 Supplemental forecast over BAC
    TCPI(BAC) 1.2174 Required remaining efficiency to meet BAC

    The project has completed less budgeted work than planned and spent more than the budgeted value of completed work. If current cost performance continues, it is forecast to finish above BAC.

    Formal EVM is strongest where predictive or hybrid control structures provide the necessary baseline measures. Adaptive delivery may use EVM at a release or other suitable performance-baseline level, but not every agile team uses formal EVM.
